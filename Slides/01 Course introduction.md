## **Reinforcement Learning** 

## **The Task** 

- You want to predict an unknown function F(X) given X; your choice of F(X) will affect the next X that will be submitted to the system (think of X as a "state" and of F(X) as an "action" that has an effect on the next state) 

- **The Performance** 

- You are given a "reward" function P that evaluates how good (or bad) was the sequence of states of X1,…,Xn; 

- **The Experience** 

   - You can run several experiments, obtaining the sequence X1,…,Xn and P(X1,...,Xn) 

## **Example** 

- **X** : your perception of your position, speed, acceleration etc. 

- **F(X)** : the action on the pedals and the handlebar 

- **P(X1,…,Xn) = -1000** if you lose your balance 

- **P(X1,…,Xn) = +10** trajectory 

if you follow your intended
